Privacy Policy for Animie Calendar

Last updated: July 2026

Animie Calendar ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and with whom it may be shared.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not require user registration or login, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personal contact information directly.

2. Data Collected Automatically

The app uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical data from your device in order to provide advertising, analytics, and crash-reporting functionality:

  • Device or other identifiers (Advertising ID): Used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads within the app. You can reset or limit use of your advertising ID at any time in your device's system settings.
  • App activity and analytics data: We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app (e.g. screens viewed, features used, general app performance) so we can improve the app.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics: We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash logs and diagnostic information when the app encounters an error, so we can identify and fix bugs.
  • Purchase history: If you buy a premium subscription or remove ads through Google Play Billing, purchase information (such as the product purchased) is processed to unlock the corresponding features and is also logged for analytics purposes.

This data is transmitted to and shared with Google (Google AdMob and Google Firebase) for the purposes described above. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. App Functionality

The app retrieves publicly available anime schedule data from external APIs.

When users tap certain content, the app opens YouTube in a browser or the YouTube application.

We are not responsible for YouTube's privacy practices.

4. Internet Usage

The app requires internet access to fetch anime data, display ads, and sync analytics/crash data described above.

5. Your Choices

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier from your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Since the app does not use accounts, there is no in-app account or profile data to delete.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy if needed. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
